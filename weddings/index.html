Wedding questions

How many guests can attend a wedding?

Wedding seating is up to about 100 guests. For larger daytime gatherings, tell us your numbers and we'll confirm what works for your event.

Can we bring our own vendors?

Yes. We have a flexible vendor policy, so you can bring the caterer, photographer, and DJ you love.

Is alcohol allowed?

Alcohol can be served with approval. There's a $200 alcohol fee, and the rules are covered in your rental agreement.

How close are the Rexburg temples?

Both Rexburg temples are about 15 minutes away, so a temple sealing in the morning and a celebration at The Burland that afternoon is easy.
