Questions, answered

Booking and pricing

How does booking work?

Pick your dates in our booking planner and you'll see an instant estimate. Send it to us and we'll confirm your date personally, usually within one business day. We then email a short rental details form and your agreement through SignNow. Your date is reserved once the agreement is signed and the payment due at signing is received. Nothing is booked automatically.

What does it cost?

Off-season (October through April) is $800 a night Monday through Thursday and $1,000 a night Friday through Sunday, with holiday nights at $1,300. Peak season (May through September) is $1,200 a weeknight and $1,500 a weekend night. Off-season weekdays can also be rented by the hour. We confirm every total in writing.

Is there a minimum stay?

Off-season weeknights have no minimum. Off-season weekends need 2 nights and holidays need 3. Every peak-season stay has a 2-night minimum.

What are the payment terms?

50% is due when you book and the rest is due 90 days before you arrive. There's also a $1,000 refundable security deposit. After your stay you get $750 back if there's no damage, since $250 covers cleaning.

How far ahead should we book?

Summer weekends go first, especially during Yellowstone season and around Pioneer Days in late July. Reach out 6 to 12 months ahead for peak dates.

Can we tour The Burland first?

Yes, and we encourage it. Request a visit through our contact page and we'll find a time that works.

Your stay

How many people can stay overnight?

The Burland sleeps 40+ on site across private family suites, so most families fit under one roof.

Can we cook our own food?

Absolutely. The full kitchen is yours and it's set up for big-group cooking. Cook together, do a potluck, or bring a caterer. We don't provide catering.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in begins at 3:00 p.m. and check-out is 11:00 a.m.

Is it good for grandparents and little kids?

Yes. The Burland is designed for all ages and the wedding spaces are ADA accessible. Kids get their own game room with ping-pong, air hockey, and an arcade corner. Tell us about any specific accessibility needs and we'll do our best to help.

Weddings and events

How many guests can The Burland hold?

Up to 130 guests in the building at one time, with seating for weddings of up to about 100. Overnight, it sleeps 40+.

How many guests can attend a wedding?

Wedding seating is up to about 100 guests, and the building holds up to 130 at one time.

Can we bring our own vendors?

Yes. We have a flexible vendor policy, so you can bring the caterer, photographer, and DJ you love.

Is alcohol allowed?

Alcohol can be served with approval. There's a $200 alcohol fee, and the rules are covered in your rental agreement.

How close are the Rexburg temples?

Both Rexburg temples are about 15 minutes away, so a temple sealing in the morning and a celebration at The Burland that afternoon is easy.

The area

How far is Yellowstone?

Yellowstone's West Entrance is a little over an hour's drive north on US-20, through Ashton and Island Park.

What is there to do nearby?

Plenty. The St. Anthony Sand Dunes are about 15 minutes away, the Henry's Fork runs through town, and Mesa Falls, Harriman State Park, the Ashton-Tetonia Trail, and Yellowstone Bear World are all an easy drive.
