A sample weekend

What a Burland reunion looks like

Friday evening

Arrive and settle in

Families roll in after 3:00, claim their suites, and gather for a welcome dinner while the kids discover the game room.

Saturday morning

Pick your adventure

Head up US-20 for a Yellowstone day, or stay close with the sand dunes 15 minutes away and Mesa Falls under an hour away.

Saturday afternoon

Dunes, river, or downtime

Sled the dunes, cool off at the Sand Bar on the Henry's Fork, or nap. Nobody's judging.

Saturday evening

The big dinner

Everyone around the ballroom tables, then games, stories, and a slideshow of old photos while the teens take over the arcade.

Sunday morning

Brunch, photos, goodbyes

One more meal together, a family photo under the chandeliers, and the kind of goodbye that takes two hours.

Local tip

Plan around Pioneer Days

Every year in late July, around July 24, St. Anthony hosts Fremont County Pioneer Days with a parade looping through downtown and a rodeo at the county fairgrounds. Local families have planned reunions around it for generations.

Those weekends book early. If you want Pioneer Days, reach out 6 to 12 months ahead.

Reunion questions

What does it cost?

Off-season (October through April) is $800 a night Monday through Thursday and $1,000 a night Friday through Sunday, with holiday nights at $1,300. Peak season (May through September) is $1,200 a weeknight and $1,500 a weekend night. Off-season weekdays can also be rented by the hour. We confirm every total in writing.

How far ahead should we book?

Summer weekends go first, especially during Yellowstone season and around Pioneer Days in late July. Reach out 6 to 12 months ahead for peak dates.

Can we tour The Burland first?

Yes, and we encourage it. Request a visit through our contact page and we'll find a time that works.

How many people can stay overnight?

The Burland sleeps 40+ on site across private family suites, so most families fit under one roof.

Can we cook our own food?

Absolutely. The full kitchen is yours and it's set up for big-group cooking. Cook together, do a potluck, or bring a caterer. We don't provide catering.

Is it good for grandparents and little kids?

Yes. The Burland is designed for all ages and the wedding spaces are ADA accessible. Kids get their own game room with ping-pong, air hockey, and an arcade corner. Tell us about any specific accessibility needs and we'll do our best to help.
