With over 10,000 square feet across three floors, there's space to be together and space to rest. Bring the whole family tree.

Right outside the door

Eastern Idaho is the best backyard around

Sand dunes 15 minutes west, a world-famous trout river through town, waterfalls up the road, and Yellowstone a little over an hour north.

St. Anthony Sand Dunes

About 10,600 acres of white quartz sand, with some dunes rising around 400 feet, just 8 miles west of town.

Upper Mesa Falls drops about 114 feet across a roughly 200-foot-wide ledge. Lower Mesa Falls adds another 65.

A little history

A downtown building with a long memory

St. Anthony grew up on the banks of the Henry's Fork, and its downtown boomed after the railroad arrived in 1899. Since 2020, this 1912 building has been rebuilt from the roof down so families can make new memories in it.

1890
Around when C.H. Moon settled here and named the town after St. Anthony Falls in Minnesota.
1893
Fremont County was created, and St. Anthony became the county seat.
1899
The railroad reached town, and downtown boomed.
1912
The Burland building went up in downtown St. Anthony.

Quick answers

How does booking work?

Pick your dates in our booking planner and you'll see an instant estimate. Send it to us and we'll confirm your date personally, usually within one business day. We then email a short rental details form and your agreement through SignNow. Your date is reserved once the agreement is signed and the payment due at signing is received. Nothing is booked automatically.

What does it cost?

Off-season (October through April) is $800 a night Monday through Thursday and $1,000 a night Friday through Sunday, with holiday nights at $1,300. Peak season (May through September) is $1,200 a weeknight and $1,500 a weekend night. Off-season weekdays can also be rented by the hour. We confirm every total in writing.

How many people can stay overnight?

The Burland sleeps 40+ on site across private family suites, so most families fit under one roof.

Can we cook our own food?

Absolutely. The full kitchen is yours and it's set up for big-group cooking. Cook together, do a potluck, or bring a caterer. We don't provide catering.

How far is Yellowstone?

Yellowstone's West Entrance is a little over an hour's drive north on US-20, through Ashton and Island Park.
