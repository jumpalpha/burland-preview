The area

The best backyard in Eastern Idaho

St. Anthony sits right in the middle of it all: sand dunes to the west, a famous trout river through town, and waterfalls and Yellowstone up the road.

About 15 minutes

St. Anthony Sand Dunes

About 10,600 acres of white quartz sand, with some dunes rising around 400 feet, just 8 miles west of town.

Good for: Kids, Teens, ATV and UTV riders, Photographers

A little over an hour to the West Entrance

Yellowstone National Park

Take US-20 north through Ashton and Island Park, over Targhee Pass, and you're at Yellowstone's West Entrance.

Good for: Everyone, Grandparents (lots of boardwalks), Wildlife fans

About 40 minutes

Upper and Lower Mesa Falls

Upper Mesa Falls drops about 114 feet across a roughly 200-foot-wide ledge. Lower Mesa Falls adds another 65.

Good for: Everyone, Grandparents, Photographers

Right through town

Henry's Fork of the Snake River

One of the West's famous trout rivers runs right through St. Anthony.

Good for: Anglers, Swimmers, Paddlers, Evening walks

About 50 minutes

Harriman State Park

The old Harriman family Railroad Ranch, inside an 11,000-acre wildlife refuge.

Good for: Wildlife fans, Bikers, Easy hikes

About 20 minutes to the Ashton trailhead

Ashton-Tetonia Trail

A 29.6-mile rail trail across historic trestles over Fall River, Conant Creek, and Bitch Creek.

Good for: Bikers, Walkers, Teens

About 25 minutes

Yellowstone Bear World

A drive-through wildlife park just south of Rexburg. Bears out the window, guaranteed.

Good for: Little kids, Rainy days

Varies

Winter in Fremont County

Island Park's hundreds of miles of groomed snowmobile trail, Kelly Canyon skiing, and snowy Mesa Falls.

Good for: Snowmobilers, Skiers, Cozy weekends
