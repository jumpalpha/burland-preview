Payment and policies

How payment works

  • 50% due when you book
  • Remaining balance due 90 days before arrival
  • $1,000 refundable security deposit. After your stay you get $750 back if there's no damage, since $250 covers cleaning.
  • Check-in begins at 3:00 p.m. and check-out is 11:00 a.m.
  • Every total is confirmed in writing before you sign.

Pricing questions

How does booking work?

Pick your dates in our booking planner and you'll see an instant estimate. Send it to us and we'll confirm your date personally, usually within one business day. We then email a short rental details form and your agreement through SignNow. Your date is reserved once the agreement is signed and the payment due at signing is received. Nothing is booked automatically.

What does it cost?

Off-season (October through April) is $800 a night Monday through Thursday and $1,000 a night Friday through Sunday, with holiday nights at $1,300. Peak season (May through September) is $1,200 a weeknight and $1,500 a weekend night. Off-season weekdays can also be rented by the hour. We confirm every total in writing.

Is there a minimum stay?

Off-season weeknights have no minimum. Off-season weekends need 2 nights and holidays need 3. Every peak-season stay has a 2-night minimum.

What are the payment terms?

50% is due when you book and the rest is due 90 days before you arrive. There's also a $1,000 refundable security deposit. After your stay you get $750 back if there's no damage, since $250 covers cleaning.

How far ahead should we book?

Summer weekends go first, especially during Yellowstone season and around Pioneer Days in late July. Reach out 6 to 12 months ahead for peak dates.

Can we tour The Burland first?

Yes, and we encourage it. Request a visit through our contact page and we'll find a time that works.
